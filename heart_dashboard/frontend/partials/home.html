Education

What is heart disease?

Heart disease is the broad term for conditions affecting the structure and function of the heart muscle. It is the leading cause of death worldwide — yet many cases are preventable with early detection.

🫀
Coronary Heart Disease
Fatty plaque builds up in the arteries supplying blood to the heart. Often undetected until angina or a heart attack occurs.
Most common
⚡
Arrhythmias
Abnormal heart rhythms from the heart's electrical system — palpitations, dizziness, shortness of breath, or cardiac arrest.
Electrical
💧
Heart Failure
When the heart muscle cannot pump blood effectively — causing fatigue, breathlessness, and swelling in the legs and abdomen.
Chronic
🔧
Valve Disease
When heart valves fail to open or close properly — disrupting blood flow and causing chest pain, breathlessness, and fatigue.
Structural
